VentWean Sentinel / Respiratory Care

Ventilator weaning indices

Calculate RSBI, minute ventilation, compliance and the CROP index from respiratory measurements. Review reported bedside considerations without producing an automated extubation decision.

Research and education only. This is not a validated clinical decision-support device. Values do not establish SBT eligibility or extubation safety; use a clinician-led protocol.

Input measurements

Only respiratory rate and tidal volume are required. Leave other inputs blank when unavailable. Do not enter patient identifiers.

Core measurements
Gas exchange (optional)

Estimated PAO₂ assumes 760 mmHg barometric pressure, 47 mmHg water vapor pressure and respiratory quotient 0.8.

Reported bedside considerations (optional)

These items are not a validated checklist or an SBT outcome assessment.

Computed results

Calculated locally in your browser. No server submission, account, or saved clinical record.

Enter measurements and select “Calculate indices” to view results.

Evidence: AARC 2024 SBT guideline advises that RSBI is not necessary to determine readiness for an SBT. The original Yang–Tobin study describes RSBI and CROP as historical predictive indices.

Privacy: The static application runs without a backend and does not transmit or persist form data. Exports are generated on your device. Avoid entering patient-identifiable data.
